“≥” marks incomplete visible counter coverage without a reset, such as an early tail or missing reading. Reset rows are marked separately because their reconstructed subtotals are uncertain. “≈” marks a rough estimate, excluded from recorded-energy totals; it is not a guaranteed minimum. The label below each estimate shows which method was used. “No early flag” does not guarantee a complete counter.

How the estimated totals are calculated

The next charging state may supply a later time and battery percentage, but usually no final kWh reading. A later charging session starts a new counter. A higher battery percentage alone cannot establish how many kWh were delivered.

Where an AC session has a terminal state and enough measured counter history, the page extends its measured energy rate over the missing time and checks the result against its measured energy per SOC point. If that test is unavailable, it may use comparable, clean sessions for the same vehicle and charger type (AC or DC) in the selected Ford JSON to convert SOC gain to kWh. It adds a missing-tail estimate to a usable counter subtotal; after a counter reset or with no usable reading, it estimates the whole session from its full SOC gain. Reference count and observed kWh per SOC point are included in the charging CSV. The page leaves estimates unavailable when its evidence checks fail. SOC rounding, charging losses and changing conditions make either method approximate, not a meter reading or a guaranteed minimum.

03 / Travel

Distance and movement

What this can and cannot count

Odometer changes show distance, including days with substantial movement. Ignition intervals and coarse GPS points do not form a verified trip log. This report does not infer top speed or driving kWh/100 km without direct, interpretable measurements.

How to read these plots

The energy/SOC ratio is a consistency check, not measured pack capacity. Whole-percent SOC, missing counter tails, battery conditioning and the unknown counter measurement point limit it. The dashboard range is an estimate that can change with recent driving and conditions; a dip or rebound alone does not show degradation or recovery. A credible SoH figure needs a battery-management diagnostic or a controlled capacity test with a suitable baseline.

Observed JSON structure and schema

The files contain an attrList array. Each entry has attrName, attrValue, categoryCode, seqNum and tableName. Raw values remain strings, even for numbers and dates. This is an observed schema, not an official Ford contract.
